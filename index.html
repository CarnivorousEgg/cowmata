Cow Mata

We are a group of like-minded people who intend to support the welfare of Cows, Pigeons etc. We are not part of any organization/society/institution. Anyone from Chennai who wishes to support for this noble cause, please do let us know. He/She can visit and help us in distribution of fodder (no financial help). If anyone wishes to help, then they can directly buy the stuffs from vendors or reach out to us. There is absolutely zero overhead, whatever is spent goes directly to the stomach of the needy animals.
